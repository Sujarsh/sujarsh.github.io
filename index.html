Unit 1: Introduction to Financial System

  • Meaning and definition of financial system
  • Objectives: Savings, investment, growth
  • Role in economic development

MCQs

1. Primary function of financial system is to mobilise savings.

2. Judiciary is not a component of financial system.

Answer Key & Explanation

Q1: Mobilise savings – It channels surplus funds to deficit units.

Q2: Judiciary – It is not part of the financial system.

Unit 2: Models of Financial System

  • Bank-based financial system
  • Market-based financial system
  • Mixed financial system (India)

MCQs

1. India follows mixed financial system.

2. USA follows market-based financial system.

Answer Key & Explanation

Q1: Mixed – Both banks and markets play roles in India.

Q2: USA – Capital markets dominate finance.

Unit 3: Structure of Indian Financial System

  • Financial institutions
  • Financial markets
  • Regulators and services

MCQs

1. RBI regulates banking system.

2. Merchant banking is a financial service.

Answer Key & Explanation

Q1: RBI – Central bank and regulator.

Q2: Merchant banking – Advisory service.

Unit 4: Financial Institutions in India

  • RBI and Commercial Banks
  • Development banks (NABARD, SIDBI)
  • NBFCs and Insurance companies

MCQs

1. NABARD is a development financial institution.

2. NBFCs are regulated by RBI.

Answer Key & Explanation

Q1: NABARD – Rural and agriculture finance.

Q2: RBI – Regulates NBFCs.

MCQs
